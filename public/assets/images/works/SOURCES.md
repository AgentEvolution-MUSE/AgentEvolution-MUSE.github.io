# Representative-work assets and metadata

Audited 2026-11-26. See `docs/representative-works-audit.md` and the adjacent JSON evidence manifest for all 53 works, manuscript reference fields and per-work primary-source links.

## Publication links

11 links are copied verbatim from BibTeX URL fields; 27 use the manuscript’s arXiv identifiers; 15 were resolved from citation metadata. Generative Agents and MemPrompt now use the cited conference publication pages.

Exact work identity is distinct from exact edition/version. MYCIN currently links the 1976 original while the manuscript cites the 2012 reprint; ALICE has different first-online/book dates; HarnessBank and SAD have revision differences. These are recorded in the audit and disclosed in tooltips. OpenReview forum pages return verification challenges, so their exact bibliography URLs were retained and paper identity checked against matching primary papers; forum contents have not been independently verified.

## Visual marks

44 retained modern-work marks come from `MUSE_Arxiv/figs/theoretical_framework/spectrum_representative_work.pdf`, SHA-256 `368e685ec8362701232ed01f8933269f268769c0a6790fefde5f9ed50402a6b5`.

39 PNGs retain native embedded pixels and alpha masks; five SVGs preserve original vector paths (Evo-Memory, SCoRe, SkillMentor, COVE, OpenSkill). They display at 32 CSS pixels without cropping.

The original AnnaAgent image incorrectly used the US Northeastern University seal. The paper lists Chinese Northeastern University. That asset was replaced with `neu-china.svg` from https://svglogo.top/library/school/NEU.svg, visually checked against https://www.neu.edu.cn/xygk/ddbs.htm. The blue mountain-and-river emblem includes 1923 and the Chinese/English university names. Original vector paths and colors are preserved; only the decorative drop shadow and excess padding were removed. It contains no embedded raster image. This is a third-party vector asset, not a direct university download. AgentEvolver uses a neutral bot pictogram: the paper identifies Tongyi Lab, and the inherited Qwen product mark does not establish a Qwen team affiliation. The seven historical systems also use generic Lucide pictograms. Generic icons are not institutional logos.
